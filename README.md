# SWEN360L-2---Lab-Exercise-02
## Redha Karashi F2500122

My project idea would be a website that would show you what the hubble telescope saw on your birthday. 
The Hubble telescope is a large space telescope launched by NASA in 1990, and take pictures and videos 
of space such as stars, planets, etc. The idea of my website is that there would be a search bar, that would 
require users to enter a date. When they enter the date, it would then render an image of what the hubble telescope
took on that date. For example, my birthday is in July 3 2005, if I enter that date it would show me a picture of a star,
which is a picture that the Hubble telescope took on that day.
